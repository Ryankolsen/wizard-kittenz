Somebody let the kittens into the dungeon. This was a mistake. Play anyway.

WIZARD KITTENZ is a dungeon crawler starring the craziest cats you've ever recruited. Solo or with friends. Your call. Pick a class and go get absolutely destroyed by a sentient vacuum cleaner. Alone if you trust no one. Or with up to 3 friends via a 5-character room code if you'd rather share the blame. No accounts. No friction.

CHOOSE YOUR DEGENERATE
Battle Kitten. Wizard Kitten. Sleepy Kitten. Chonk Kitten. Four starting classes each with its own skill tree and stats. Level up. Spec into spells or steel. Unlock upgraded tiers as you go.

GO IT ALONE OR DON'T
Solo play is fully supported offline and first class. No internet needed. No party required. No witnesses. Progress saves locally and syncs the moment you reconnect. Feeling social? Or just want backup? Real time co op for up to 4 players is right there too and syncs instantly.

MEET THE FLOOR BOSSES (THEY ALL HATE YOU)
An Angry Pigeon in a tattered cloak who has decided today is the day.
A Dog Knight in full plate. One gauntlet on a greatsword. The other on a bottle of mead.
A Catnip Dealer mouse with a trench coat full of glowing green bags. He has what you need. You don't want what he has.
A Rogue Roomba possessed by dark magic and terrible pathfinding.
A Haunted Spray Bottle. Every cat knows this is the real final boss.
And presiding over all of it: THE VACUUM. Good luck.

POWER UPS THAT MAKE POOR DECISIONS FOR YOU
Chug some Ale for a strength boost and a wobble you can't control. Snort catnip for a speed buff you'll regret. Eat a mushroom and watch your spells fire themselves. Nothing here is a good idea. Everything here is fun.

FREE TO PLAY. ACTUALLY FREE.
No ads ever. The only thing for sale is a revive token for when your questionable decisions catch up with you. Buy one if you want. Ignore it if you don't.

Rated Mature for cartoon violence and comedic alcohol/catnip/mushroom theming. It's a kitten dungeon crawler not a lecture.

Grab your yarn ball of choice. The dungeon isn't going to loot itself.
